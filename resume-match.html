    Skill-by-skill breakdown & how the score works
    The matcher scans ~90 skills (with aliases — “postgres” counts as SQL, “powerbi” as Power BI) in both texts, ignoring negated phrases like no experience with X. Score = covered / (covered + missing) where covered skills are weighted by how often and how prominently they appear in your resume (a “Skills” section adds weight, repeats up to ×1.75), and missing skills are penalized more when the posting says required / must have (×1.25), less when it says nice to have / preferred (×0.6), and less again for soft skills. It’s a heuristic, not a verdict — use it to decide what to add, not whether to apply.
